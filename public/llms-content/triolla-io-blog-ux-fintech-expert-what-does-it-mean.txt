UX Fintech Expert, what does it mean?


As a team of domain experts with extensive knowledge and experience in our field, we have completed more than 50 projects in fintech apps & platforms, giving us a unique perspective and in-depth understanding of the product design process. Our expertise can be invaluable in helping you avoid costly mistakes and achieve success with your product design. Our team’s experience and knowledge can be a crucial factor in ensuring that your product design process is as efficient and effective as possible.


At Triolla, we take our job seriously. One of our leading concepts in developing phenomenal products for our clients is the Vertical Segmentation Expertise concept. In this article, we would like to briefly introduce you to the notion that allows us to “make the magic happen” time after time.


In terms of technological advancements, the Fintech industry has undergone a radical transformation in the past decade. The term “financial technology” refers to a wide range of digital tools that facilitate analytical and financial transactions. “Fintech,” as it is commonly known, usually refers to online banking, foreign exchange, and cryptocurrency trading. In many Fintech apps, especially the banking and investment sector, that strive to make finance more accessible to the public, good user experience is a vital feature.


Fintech UX design requires certain characteristics in order to provide a secure and enjoyable user experience that leads to fast adoption & the overall success.


Trust and confidenceHow to combine creativity, innovation, and still project trust & confidence; multi-step approvals and encrypted data storage are some of the must-have security measures. A behind-the-scenes armory cannot assure users that the app is safe.


Simplified customer journeyUsers often experience higher levels of stress or tension when using financial or fintech apps. Therefore, it is important to design a seamless customer journey in any fintech app or platform. This can be achieved by creating simple flows that even unsophisticated users can easily understand and adopt. When the user experience is straightforward, it can help reduce anxiety, increase confidence and build trust. These are all crucial factors to consider when developing and launching a new fintech app or platform.


Avoid Data OverloadHaving too much data can cause tension, impatience, and a poor overall user experience. As a result, it is important for product designers in the fintech industry to strike a balance between simplifying data consumption for all users and allowing more advanced users to delve deeper if they choose. This can be achieved through user research to identify the most frequently accessed information and determine which data should be more readily available versus being “under the hood” and requiring additional steps to access.

Retention, retention & retentionRetention is a measure of the success of your platform. If retention is low, it indicates that the app is not delivering the value that users expected. To improve retention, a skilled product designer will include various features that encourage users to continue using the app. This will help ensure that users find the app valuable and continue to use it regularly.


To effectively engage users, it is important to customize the value proposition for each user persona. This helps to reduce cognitive load by anticipating the needs of the user and presenting relevant experiences automatically. When users feel like the app is tailored specifically to them, they are more likely to remain engaged. The most successful fintech apps are able to adapt to the needs of each individual user, ensuring that they feel understood while also avoiding information overload.


Fintech companies can improve customer engagement and retention by delivering personalized user experiences through data insights and a user-first approach informed by big data. However, they must also consider the balance between personalization and optimization of user interactions, including tone, aesthetics, and general “how” and “when” questions. Personalized offers and support can increase customer retention and brand loyalty.


FeedbackTo be effective, support must be immediate and actionable. An excellent support system can help users feel in control at all times and is an important factor in creating a positive experience.


All-in-one marketplacesGoing to a local bank branch to conduct business is a thing of the past. Thanks to third-party integrations, personal finance apps can do more than just transfer money. Various financial services are available right at the fingertips of users. There is something for everyone, whether you’re an average Joe or a financial expert.


Improved security appearanceImproved security in fintech apps can greatly enhance the user experience. When users feel that their personal and financial information is secure, they are more likely to trust the app and feel comfortable using it. This can lead to increased engagement and retention. By implementing security features such as secure login procedures, encryption of sensitive data, and regular security updates, fintech companies can show their commitment to protecting their users’ information and create a safer experience for users.
